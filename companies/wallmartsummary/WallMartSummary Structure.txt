WALL MART SUMMARY — WORKFLOW STRUCTURE

INPUT
  Upload one Wal Mart inquiry workbook.
  Required business columns:
    Acct | Div | Name | Ref# | Date | Due Date | Order#
    Current | 1-30 Days | 31-60 Days | 61-90 Days | Over 90 Days
    Claim# | Comment

PREPARATION
  Upload rows
    |
    +--> Preserve original business columns and extra columns
    +--> Create / refresh Ref Type from Ref# (IN, DB, or CR)
    +--> Create / refresh Total from all ageing buckets
    +--> Prepare Date and Due Date as MM/DD/YYYY

OUTPUT
  WALL_MART_STATUS_SUMMARY.xlsx
    |
    +--> WallMartSummary_Sheet: prepared uploaded rows
    +--> Invoices_Status: approved invoice status layout
    +--> DBs_Status: approved open-aging DB status layout

RULES
  - File-only processing; the upload/report route does not use SQL Server.
  - Status totals include WC9085, WA9085, SA9200, and WM9263 only.
  - Generated source-data sheets are normal Excel Tables.
  - Workbook metadata Creator and Last Modified By: AnsSys & openpyxl.
