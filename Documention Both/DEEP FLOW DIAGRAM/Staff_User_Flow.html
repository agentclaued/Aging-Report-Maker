Overstock Staff User Flow

This is the simple HTML version for staff and non-technical users. It shows what the app is for, what each step means, and where the final results go.

Purpose

The app helps turn Overstock inquiry, invoice, and payment files into matched reports.

Simple Workflow

flowchart TD A["Open the Overstock app"] --> B["Upload inquiry file"] B --> D["Upload invoice workbooks"] D --> E["Upload payment detail workbooks"] E --> F["Run matching steps"] F --> G["Run summary and credit steps"] G --> H["Download final report files"]

What Each Step Means

  • Step 01: loads the inquiry file and prepares it for the system.
  • Step 02: combines invoice workbooks into one clean invoice dataset.
  • Step 03: combines payment detail workbooks into one clean payment dataset.
  • Step 06: handles credit note, debit number, unapplied credit, and deduction logic.
  • Step 07A: builds summary reports.
  • Step 07: builds the final credit applied result.

Main Folders Staff Should Know

  • output/: final generated files are saved here.
  • duplicate_staging/: temporary holding area when the app finds the same file name again.
  • templates/: the app screen layout lives here.
  • companies/overstock/: Overstock-specific setup rules live here.
The app takes multiple spreadsheet files, cleans them, checks how they match, applies credit/debit logic, and gives the user finished report files to download.
